Exercise 5 solution: sum_allreduce.c

MPI_Scatter distributes the input chunks, and MPI_Allreduce with MPI_SUM computes the global total on every rank. Each rank prints its local sum, the shared total, and its percentage contribution. Root also checks the result against 500000500000.

Build and run with: mpicc -O2 -o sum_allreduce Exercise05/sum_allreduce.c && mpirun -np 4 ./sum_allreduce
